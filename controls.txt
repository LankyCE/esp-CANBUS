Flash helper usage

Build + flash + monitor (auto port):
.\flash.ps1

Build + flash + monitor (specific port):
.\flash.ps1 -Port COM6

Skip build, flash + monitor:
.\flash.ps1 -NoBuild -Port COM6

Build + flash only (no monitor):
.\flash.ps1 -NoMonitor -Port COM5

Safe monitor after reset:
.\monitor-safe.ps1 -Port COM5 -DurationSeconds 30

setup envior
$env:IDF_TOOLS_PATH='<PATH_TO>\.espressif'; $env:IDF_PATH='<PATH_TO>\.espressif\v5.5.3\esp-idf'; . "$env:IDF_PATH\export.ps1"
